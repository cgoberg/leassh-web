What Leassh runs on

Leassh is built for computers: Windows, Apple Silicon macOS, and Linux. It does not run on phones, tablets, or Chromebooks. The dashboard is local to your own server.

Why computers first

Deep local telemetry

Processes, CPU, RAM, disk, GPU, idle sessions and screen time come from the machine itself. Phone MDM APIs do not expose the same depth to a self-hosted tool.

Your network, your data

Leassh is local-first. The server, dashboard and AI analysis run on hardware you control; there is no hosted dashboard to trust with a family's history.

Phones already have good tools

Google Family Link and Apple Screen Time are the right tools for phones and tablets. Leassh covers the computers those tools do not reach, and Home Assistant can bring the signals together.

Where Leassh fits

Use Leassh for the Windows, Mac, and Linux computers in the house. Use Family Link for Android and Chromebooks, and Screen Time for iPhone and iPad. Home Assistant can combine the signals you choose to bring in; Leassh itself stays focused on computers.
